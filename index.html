About

Gokuldas Tejpal Hospital, Mumbai’s iconic government-run free hospital since 1874, continues its rich legacy under the leadership of Dean Dr. Jitendra Kamal Kashirao Deshmukh. Housed in a historic early English Gothic building, it now takes a major leap forward with the launch of a state-of-the-art Government Medical College in 2024, offering MBBS seats. Blending 150 years of compassionate service with modern innovation, GT Hospital has become a dynamic hub of healthcare and medical education, shaping skilled and compassionate healthcare professionals for Maharashtra and the nation.
